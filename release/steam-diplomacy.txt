[h1]Honor Mod Series — RimWorld 1.7[/h1]
[b]Experimental 0.2.0, verified with RimWorld 1.7.4985 unstable / Waylith and Harmony 3 for 1.7. Not for 1.6.[/b]

[b]Royalty DLC is REQUIRED ONLY for Honor.Nobility. Without Royalty, skip Nobility; subscribe to Harmony, Core, Society, Diplomacy and Oaths individually. Subscribe All includes Nobility.[/b]

Recommended order: Harmony → RimWorld / owned DLC → Honor.Core → selected expansions. Workshop subscription does not enable mods automatically. Use one copy of each module: local OR Workshop.

Core: honor Need tiers; insults lead to mandatory duels with matched weapons, arena control, immediate field tending and social memories.
Society: voluntary apology acceptance before staging; witnesses, delayed colony rumors, blood-family honor and repeated-insult rivals.
Nobility: title-based sensitivity and proxy champions. Needs Royalty.
Diplomacy: player-triggered settlement insult quests, caravan arena visits, representative duels and fixed silver/goodwill outcomes. No DLC required.
Oaths: witnessed ceremonies, five-day victory/peace oaths, mood/honor consequences and ceremonial titles. No DLC required.

Each expansion requires Core 0.2.0+ and Harmony; expansions do not require one another. Ideology is optional for custom challenger precepts. Other DLC are optional.

English and Korean follow the game language. Early experimental release: no general reward negotiation, no complete intentional-versus-accidental killing detection, no CE/large-mod-list compatibility claim. Test in a spare save. Removing persisted gameplay mods mid-save is not supported.
